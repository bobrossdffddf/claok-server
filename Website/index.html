Questions people actually ask.

Do I need a computer?
Once, to install it. After that Cloak runs from the phone alone and re-signs itself when Apple's window runs out. You could throw the computer away, though I'd rather you didn't.
Why does the installer ask for my Apple ID?
Apple only lets an app onto a phone if it's signed by an Apple ID, and it has to be yours. The sign-in goes from your computer straight to Apple. It never touches my server. You can remove the saved account from the app any time.
Does it need a jailbreak?
No. Cloak uses the location simulator that's already inside iOS for developers. Your phone stays completely stock, and stopping the simulation puts your real GPS back at once.
Will the app I'm using notice?
Mostly no, and Cloak works harder than anything else at that: real drives instead of jumps, GPS noise that behaves like real GPS, and an Exposure grade that catches the non-GPS tells (your IP, your time zone, your motion sensors). One thing no tool can hide: iOS marks a simulated fix as simulated. A handful of apps read that. Cloak lists them in the app rather than pretending.
Which iPhones and iOS versions?
Any iPhone or iPad that runs iOS 17 or later, including the iOS 26 and 27 betas. I run the beta myself, so it gets tested there first.
Does it work on cellular, away from Wi-Fi?
Yes. The link Cloak uses prefers Wi-Fi, so leaving it needs a one-time Shortcut to relink over cellular. Cloak shows you exactly when to set it up and walks you through it. After that it's automatic.
What happens if your server goes down?
Nothing you'd notice for two weeks. Your licence is a signed token the phone checks by itself, with 14 days of grace. Being offline is not a refusal.
Can I move it to a new phone?
Yes. One phone at a time per licence. Release it from Settings in the app, install on the new phone, paste the same key.
Is it on the App Store?
No, and it can't be. A location simulator would never pass review. That's why the installer exists, and why updates come from inside the app instead.
